# B16-A2 conditional residual VAE

This method keeps the frozen seed-42 E7 prediction as a full-rank dense bypass and
adds a bounded, zero-initialized residual correction generated by a 64-dimensional
conditional VAE.

- The posterior receives only train-row E7 residual targets and observation masks.
- The conditional prior receives only frozen E7 metadata/structure context.
- Validation and deployable inference call `forward_prior_mean(context)` only.
- Epoch 0 is the exact E7 prediction and participates in early stopping as the safe
  fallback.
- The optimized loss retains protein-space masked absolute loss, train-only paired
  delta loss, KL warmup, and per-dimension free bits.
- The additional trainable parameter count must remain at or below 1.5 million.
